      Reading this page
      
      
      
        
      PR-AUC
      Area under the precision-recall curve for the leukemic class. Model-selection metric. 0.667 = guessing (class prevalence).
      
        
      ROC-AUC
      Probability a random leukemic cell scores above a random normal cell. 0.5 = guessing.
      
        
      Recall (ALL)
      Share of leukemic cells flagged at threshold 0.5. A miss here is the costly error.
      
        
      ECE
      Expected calibration error, 15 bins. How far stated confidence is from observed accuracy. Lower is better.
      
        
      Subject CI
      Confidence interval from resampling whole subjects, because cells from one person are not independent.
      
        
      Early stopping
      Training stops after 5 epochs (8 for the scratch CNN) without a better val PR-AUC. Best epoch is kept.
